Three ways to show the same pipeline

The problem with the last version: it rendered three different kinds of number identically — a gate pass-rate (62 of 1,217), a stock sitting still (641), and a concurrency meter (2 of 10). Each of these concepts picks one idea and commits to it. Real production numbers throughout.

A · Stock & flowsystem dynamics

Boxes are stocks (things sitting still). Between them are valves carrying a rate — items per day — with the knob that sets its ceiling. Answers “how many are in, and how fast are they moving” by never conflating the two.

B · Where everything wentmass conserved

Fixes “how can we see 0 after 1,217”. Every item that doesn’t proceed goes somewhere visible — excluded by a named gate, failed, exhausted, or still waiting. Each bar sums to its input, so nothing vanishes between rows.

C · Queue metricsLittle’s Law

No picture at all. Work in progress, the rate in, the rate out, and therefore how long a note entering now would wait. Ugly and the most informative of the three.
