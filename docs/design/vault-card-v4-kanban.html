What survived from v3

The journey strip — now eight pixels tall, no labels at rest, full skill names on hover. Narration as a single italic line. Project as foot-signature, not a chip. Witness as serif italic ("in Boris's hands"). Confidence as edge fidelity. Decay as visible fade. Failure as distressed wallpaper.

What's new at kanban scale

Title clamped to three lines. The "system has notes" coloured dot moved to inline-end of the title (rationale/question/error/result). Priority folded into the #seq line. Witness shrunk to 18px avatar + italic phrase. The whole column carries mood — Failed column has a subtle diagonal wash; Running column's header line glows.

What the eye lands on first

Title, every time. Then in a half-second: the journey strip tells you stage; the warm/hot/stale tint tells you whether you should look closer; the italic narration line tells you why. Owner is a quiet signature, not a banner. You read what the system is doing, not what the database fields say.

What's still a bet

The narration sentence needs templates per event-kind, machine-generated. Cheap to build, but it's real infrastructure. Skill labels under journey dots need short canonical names. Three-line clamp may lose nuance for long titles — alternative is letting them grow and accepting variable card height (probably right).
