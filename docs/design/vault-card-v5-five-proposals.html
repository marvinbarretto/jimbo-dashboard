Strength

Familiar to a developer. Every line is a fact. Mono everything → no visual ambiguity. Reads top-to-bottom like a deploy log.

Weakness

All cards look similar at a glance — the eye has to read text to differentiate. Less peripheral signal than a status board.

Best at

Failure triage. The narrative log lines are perfect for "what went wrong here?"

Strength

You can see twelve items on screen with full titles, no truncation. Vertical scanning is the fastest reading mode the eye has.

Weakness

Loses the kanban-column-as-status metaphor. Status is encoded by left-edge colour + pipeline segment instead. Hidden state-change affordance (no drag-into-column).

Best at

"What does today need from me?" — the over-arching daily review. Drop the kanban entirely on the execution page; keep it for grooming.

Strength

Where the work is in the pipeline becomes obvious in 100ms. The bold stage labels read at a glance, even from across the room. Failed stage highlighted in-place.

Weakness

Five 8-character cells need ~280px minimum width to stay legible — column-thin layouts struggle. Title sits below the pipeline rather than above; some readers expect title-first.

Best at

Watching the system run. When you want to know what's moving and what's stuck, the headline pipeline gives the answer immediately without reading words.
