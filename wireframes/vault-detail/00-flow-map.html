NOTES: — Greyscale is the house rule for these wireframes; the rail is the deliberate exception. Its whole premise is colour-encoded depth, so a greyscale rail can't be judged. Everything else stays grey. — All 17 projects now carry a color_token (backfilled 2026-07-22), so the rail always has a hue to use. — Epic alpha is derived from the project hue, NOT a second token. An epic has no colour of its own — that's what makes the rail read as containment rather than as three unrelated tags. — OPEN: at 5px × 3 bars the rail is ~25px wide. Enough to recognise a project by colour once you know the palette, not enough to distinguish #7ac4c4 PMQ Bingo from #7ac4a4 Film Planner cold. The breadcrumb text carries the real identity; the rail carries the *shape*. Is that the right split?
