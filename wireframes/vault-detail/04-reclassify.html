GOAL: Let an operator change what an item IS, or retire it, without ever guessing what a button does. PERSONA: Marvin, who pressed "→ note" purely to find out what it did and then couldn't tell whether he'd broken the item. That is the exact failure this screen exists to prevent. PRIMARY: Cancel. The safe exit is the default on a destructive surface. SUCCESS: Every option states its consequence in the operator's language before he commits, and anything reversible says so.
Reclassify #3365currently: task · active · ungroomed
Keep as a task Stays dispatchable. Can be groomed, scored, and handed to an agent. — no change
Make it a note Stops being dispatchable — leaves the grooming board and the execution queue, and no agent will pick it up. Keeps its project, epic, tags and history. Reversible.
Make it a reference Something to read later, not to do. Same as a note but shows in reference lists.
Archive Hidden from every board and list. Nothing is lost, and you can restore it. Reversible.
Delete permanently The item, its thread, and its history are gone. This cannot be undone. Type the item number to confirm.
Cancel Apply
