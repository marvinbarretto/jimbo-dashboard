Data wrinkles this design must absorb:
1. Fragmentation — "1 pale ale", "3 pale ale", "1 half pint IPA", "1 punk ipa" are separate frequent rows for near-the-same thing; the granola family has three variants. v1: strip the leading quantity for display and let hide/pin handle the rest. v2: light server-side stemming in /frequent.
2. Time-of-day — granola is a morning usual, beer an evening one. v2: rank by frequency within ±3h of now (?at= param on /frequent) so the row reorders itself through the day. The two-row drinks/food split covers most of this for free in v1.
3. Drift — macros ride the *latest* log of that label, so correcting one entry's kcal in the ledger silently updates the chip. That's a feature; note it in the sheet copy if it surprises.
