Week / month page — consistency

Lives on /journal/overview/week/:week, which today renders nothing check-related. Fed by GET /api/day-checks/{id}/history — already built, currently unused by the dashboard. Deliberately not a streak counter: one miss shouldn’t zero anything, and a gap is missing data rather than a “no”.

Grid decisions.
  • “Kept 13 / 30” instead of a streak. A streak counter punishes one miss and you stop opening the page.
  • Empty cells are outlined, not red. The schema already treats absence as unanswered — the visual has to agree.
  • “hit 4 of 4” weeks is the honest scoreboard for a 3/wk target — 13/30 looks like failure until you know the target.
