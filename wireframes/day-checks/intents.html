Modelling intent — five visions

Jimbo already holds a self-model nobody's days are connected to: values → priorities → goals → experiments, each with confidence and a review date. It also holds what you actually did, in day_check_entries. Nothing joins them. An intent is the candidate edge.

These five differ on one question: how much of a thing is an intent? A field on a row, a first-class entity with its own library, or a hypothesis you're testing. Each mock shows the day surface it produces, the schema it needs, and what it costs. All five fix the identity bug — an occurrence attaches to a stable id, never to a string.

Buys
Zero friction at the moment that matters. Meaning gets fished out in conversation — which is the one mechanism your own data shows actually works — and the tray only nags about things that have earned it.

Nothing is intentional at the time. You wanted the UI to register intent as you live it; this registers it in hindsight.
Watch
The tray is a queue, and queues grow. Needs a cheap “leave it alone” that sticks.
