Notes — the day's shape
  • This is the “progress of what's happened so far today” you asked for, and the reason it must be cumulative rather than the per-hour bars: bars show distribution, a rising line shows accumulation against expectation.

Notes — the time-of-day rule
  • Comparisons on a live day are time-truncated. At 08:30 the page compares against yesterday at 08:30, not yesterday's total — otherwise every morning reads as catastrophic failure.
  • The delta label says so explicitly (“vs yesterday at 08:30”) so the reader is never guessing which comparison they're looking at. On a past day the qualifier disappears.
  • This is the single most important correctness rule in the whole redesign and the easiest to get wrong.
