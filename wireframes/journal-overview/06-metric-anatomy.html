Notes — the rules that fall out
  • Percentage vs absolute. Absolute when the unit is human-readable and the base is small (“+4 commits”); percentage when the base is large or the unit is time (“−38%”). Not a per-caller choice — the primitive decides, so the rail stays consistent.
  • “From zero” never shows a percentage. +∞% is not a fact. It reads “+6 · none yesterday”.
  • Absent ≠ zero, everywhere. Em-dash and a reason, never a 0 and a red arrow.
  • Inversion is per-metric, declared by the caller ([higherIsBetter]="false"). YouTube and sleep-debt break any global rule.
  • No colour decisions in this wireframe. Arrows carry direction here; whether the production version also tints is a visual pass, deliberately deferred.
Notes — what this means for ui-stat-card
  • Do not mutate stat-card into this. It has a second, legitimate job: text facts (value="Manual · operator-intake" in vault-detail, nutrition totals). A 30px numeral treatment would wreck those.
  • So: ui-metric is new, ui-stat-card is narrowed to fact-tiles and loses its ::ng-deep flex patch. Two clean primitives instead of one overloaded one.
