The diagnosis these respond to
Wordy → the prompt spends a third of its length on anti-hallucination hedging; caution pads prose. Insights in #206 average 80+ words each.
Unexplained questions → the rationale exists (`gates`, plus the model's reasoning) but is thrown away at generation time; only one terse line survives.
Samey → no memory of its own prior reasoning; every run re-derives the world from ~21 curls. Only real compounding loop: ▲/▼ tags over the last 4 briefings.
Underfed → interrogate tensions/contradictions/experiments, phone+sleep telemetry, mood trend, belief blocks, pending-for-marvin: all unused today.
01 · Findings Ledger analyst
Lead with 1–3 evidence-backed findings, numbered and persistent (F-041…). The bet: you'd trade all nine sections for two findings that survive scrutiny.
02 · Decision Desk chief of staff
The briefing is a queue, not a report: questions, verdicts due, 18 handoffs waiting on you. Success = decisions cleared, and it shows the cost of each day you don't.
03 · Evidence Card the mechanic you asked for
Not a layout — a component. Every question ships with "why I'm asking": signals (each hyperlinked into the dashboard), inference, confidence, and what each answer changes. Hide/reveal full working.
04 · Tension Report interrogate-powered
Organised around your standing tensions (deferral pattern, solo-viability, pivot-as-freedom-vs-avoidance). Each day: which side did yesterday actually feed? Flat, evidence-cited.
05 · Diff Briefing anti-samey
Renders ONLY what changed since the last briefing. Unchanged state collapses to one line. Wordiness solved structurally: no delta, no words.
06 · Morning Paper email + newsletters
Front-page metaphor. Dispatches column fed by newsletter subscriptions through Gmail (your idea) filtered by the interests file — UnHerd, error fares, Watford FC, no BBC sludge.
07 · Scoreboard mirror, sharpened
Numbers-first, near-zero prose. Every bucket rendered on its own clock (ship/runway/cadence). One insight sentence allowed. Everything else is a drill-down link.
08 · Companion Thread dialogue
The briefing is an opening message in a persistent thread, not a document. Reply to any section; "why are you asking?" is a first-class move; tomorrow's opener remembers.
09 · Week Shape planner-first
The unit is the week, not the day. Today's real state — eight empty days — rendered as the headline, with texture commitments treated as material, not reward.
10 · Editor's Verdict trust + compounding
The briefing grades itself: yesterday's calls vs what happened, calibration over time, what your ▲/▼ tags changed, and a provenance footer for every pipeline hop. Monitoring the hookups, made visible.
